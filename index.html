About Me

I am a hard-working and friendly person from Naogaon, Bangladesh. I completed my Class 5 from Sultanpur North GOVT High School in 2022, and Class 8 from Par Naogaon High School in 2025.

For over 2 years, I have served as a Peer Leader under UNICEF's child protection initiatives — working directly with young people in my community to raise awareness about child marriage, cyberbullying, violence, and mental health.

Beyond community work, I have a deep passion for 3D modelling using Blender. I also troubleshoot computer problems and solve technical errors, which helps me grow my skills every day.

I believe in lifelong learning and giving back to the community. I am always ready to take on new challenges and do my absolute best in everything I do.
